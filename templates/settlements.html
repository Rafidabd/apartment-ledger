Important distinction

“Known amount to follow up” is the unpaid contribution target (when set) plus outstanding personal and shared-fund advances. It is a follow-up figure, not an expense or a cash transaction. Targets are defined per month and are not automatically carried into the next month; loans do carry forward until repaid. The caretaker's household reimbursements belong to the collective fund and are shown separately in the caretaker account.
